What is a godh bharai?

Godh bharai means “filling the lap.” It is a traditional Indian celebration for a mother-to-be, where family and friends bless her and fill her lap with fruits, sweets, flowers and gifts, wishing her and the baby good health, happiness and abundance. Think of it as a baby shower wrapped in blessings, with music, good food and lots of love.
